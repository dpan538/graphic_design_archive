## Epistemic boundary

All output remains `exploratory_derived_signal`. It cannot assert or estimate
influence, causation, contact, lineage, creator intent, historical importance,
quality, canonicality, or probability of relation. Rarity is observed support,
not importance. Source concentration is corpus composition, not truth. A
governed geography match is an archive attribute match, not object distance or
historical proximity.

Embeddings, neural models, LLM/SLM processing, vector databases, learned
metrics, clustering, UMAP, and t-SNE are outside scope and absent.
