## Product distinction

```text
TRACE Exploration
├── Validated Exploration
└── Open Inquiry
```

Validated Exploration and Open Inquiry are distinct product layers. Open
Inquiry is not an opt-in expansion of validated results. Its hypotheses remain
unresolved, never generate implicit pair edges, and cannot modify the validated
graph, compositions, topology, exports, or metrics.
