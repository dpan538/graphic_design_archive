## Decision rule

The final package must issue exactly one of
`NLP_CORPUS_AUDIT_ONLY`, `NLP_BASELINE_FAMILIES_SHORTLISTED`, or
`PROVISIONAL_INTERNAL_NLP_CHANNEL_SELECTED`. It must never issue
`PUBLIC_NLP_MODEL_SELECTED`.

A provisional internal channel additionally requires complete field
governance, a reproducible public-safe corpus, full-cohort lexical and dense
evidence, passing known-pair retrieval, bounded/disclosed source and language
leakage, acceptable boilerplate robustness, measured hubness, explicit aspect
behavior, eligible licensing, and explanations. Sparse verified identity pairs
and zero verified Task B pairs materially constrain what can be claimed.

Final decision rationale: the corpus, lexical baselines, six hardened dense
encodings, metadata masking, source masking, and bounded comparisons completed,
but both dense title models triggered the frozen source-provider-dominance gate.
No reliable language-label cohort was selected, the overall hubness suite and
review packet remain incomplete, and Task B has zero verified pairs. Therefore
no baseline family, dense model, channel position, fusion result, or public
model is selected.
