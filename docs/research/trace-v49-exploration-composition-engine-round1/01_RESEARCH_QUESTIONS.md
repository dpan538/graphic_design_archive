# Research questions

1. A bounded neighbourhood is the seed-reachable, frozen-qualified candidate set at depth one or two, with at most eight nodes per semantic image and degree constrained by the selected topology.
2. Competition is resolved by ordinal Pareto-style evidence groups and minimal sufficiency, never a scalar Top-K score.
3. The six topology entry contracts are explicit and distinct; topology is an inquiry structure, not a historical fact.
4. Equal evidence at a capacity boundary and multiple valid topologies remain `UNRESOLVED`.
5. Pruning uses finite non-negative reason codes and never changes Round 14 qualification.
6. Split means that one input requires more than one bounded composition because no admitted bridge remains; it is not historical separation.
7. Evidence gaps are explicit unresolved-evidence states, not failed or negative associations.
8. Semantic output is deterministic. A seed may vary equivalent presentation only.
9. Equal node sizes, equal undirected line widths, circular peer placement, text provenance, and a leakage audit limit visual-semantic overstatement.
10. Future Context and Spacetime inputs may filter, facet, annotate, or arrange; none may override frozen association qualification without a separate research round.
