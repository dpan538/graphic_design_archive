# Limitations and open questions

- The composition corpus is bounded and researcher-authored; it is not population-level validation.
- External human domain review is incomplete.
- The degree-two rule is justified by the frozen topology budget, not by historical network structure.
- `AUTO` deliberately leaves several cases unresolved; V1 does not claim a universal topology priority.
- Support-class text may still influence perceived credibility even without visual rank and needs human evaluation.
- Circular peer placement reduces default hierarchy but cannot make a diagram rhetorically neutral.
- The 5/10/20/40-node stress suite tests deterministic bounded decomposition and output size, not product-scale performance.
- Synthetic stress inputs test mechanics only and introduce no historical claim.
- Real archive objects, Context, and Spacetime remain outside normative fixtures.
- No typed relation, causal inference, direction, chronology, hierarchy, influence, dependence, embedding, similarity model, clustering, or vector database is ready.
- Public rendering remains unsafe until external review, accessibility testing, user interpretation studies, and a separate product activation gate.
