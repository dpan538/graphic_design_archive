## Subset policy

The analysis also emits 59 source-concentration rows across the global cohort and selected governed subsets. Subset rows are permitted only at support of at least 30 public objects. Each row exposes its eligible denominator, observed object count, source assignment denominator, top counts/shares, HHI, and entropy. Small unobserved or under-supported subsets are not expanded into meaningless statistics.

Source, creator, and object type are normalized/hash-derived public metadata signals. They are therefore `LEVEL_B_DETERMINISTIC_DERIVED_STATISTIC`, not governed Level A Context features. Their normalization enables deterministic grouping but does not turn descriptive source text into a controlled vocabulary.

## Cross-dimensional source cells

The bounded pair registry includes source by medium, theme, decade, and geography. The bounded triple registry includes source-theme-decade and source-medium-geography. Every committed row is observed, denominator-bearing, and public-only. Conditional rates and lift are labeled `ANALYSIS_DIAGNOSTIC`; they are not calibrated probabilities.

## Permitted and prohibited interpretation

Permitted: identify concentration, compare release subsets that meet minimum support, audit provider dominance, and plan sampling or interface disclosure.

Prohibited: call a source dominant in the historical field, treat source share as authority or quality, infer independence between providers, or use concentration as a relation/importance score.
