# Context V1 scope

## Research question

Context V1 answers: “How does this archive describe and curate the selected record within its controlled research context?”

It does not answer: “What historical relationships did this record have?”

## Included controlled representations

- `medium`: the archive’s governed medium/format category for the selected record.
- `theme`: the archive’s governed thematic research category for the selected record.
- `movement_context`: a project-curated movement context used for research navigation.

Each visible representation has a governed term ID, representation ID, publication state, explanation code, permitted interpretation, prohibited interpretations, and safe provenance summary.

## Root metadata

The selected-record root may display the public surface ID, title, source-reported creator attribution, source-reported object type, source-reported date, and source name. These fields provide descriptive orientation; they are not Context nodes or relations.

## Excluded or deferred

- Source membership rows are explanation/provenance only.
- Typed and raw region are deferred to Spacetime.
- Raw medium strings, collection details, source identifiers and locators, descriptions, notes, and subjects are deferred to source/provenance work.
- Normalized dates and places are deferred to Spacetime.
- Images and rights are excluded from Context V1.
- Trace confidence, semantic relations, similarity, computed associations, and Exploration factors are excluded.

All 15 grouped or compound public surfaces are described as selected records. Their classifications apply to the public surface/group and must not be inherited automatically to every underlying member.
