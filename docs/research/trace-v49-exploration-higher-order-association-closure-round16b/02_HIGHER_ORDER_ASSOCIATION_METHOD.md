# Round 16B higher-order association method

## Status and correction boundary

This checkpoint defines the method; it does not claim higher-order closure. Round 16A remains a reproducible baseline for a finite pair-derived computational model. Its 31 active vocabulary concepts, 465 unordered pairs, 21 active pair associations, 58 canonical pair-edge subgraphs, 81 topology compositions, 228 production compositions, 5,760 states, 749,944 transitions, 5,760 workflows, and 11,520 exports remain measurements of that model. They are not counts of the complete association space.

The governing correction is categorical:

- an association is an evidence-bearing semantic research object;
- a composition is a governed visual or navigational realization;
- a pair association has exactly two members;
- a higher-order association has three or more members;
- a group association does not manufacture its internal pair associations;
- a connected pair graph does not manufacture group-level historical coherence;
- a state, workflow, or export is neither an association nor a composition.

The machine authority for this method is `raw/higher-order-association-method-v1.json`. Candidate and review records must conform to the new research schemas. The existing v2 production schema remains unchanged at this checkpoint.

## Candidate universe

The candidate universe is not the power set of the vocabulary and is not derived only from the 21-edge graph. It is the canonical union of finite outputs from twelve governed triggers:

1. explicit n-ary grammar;
2. same-locator multi-concept evidence;
3. compatible same-case source bundles;
4. every prior three-plus-node product or composition structure;
5. isolated active vocabulary;
6. bounded research-only senses;
7. negative and near-miss controls;
8. overlaps and reconciliation conflicts;
9. database provenance discovery;
10. adaptive external scholarly search;
11. counterevidence and falsification review;
12. recursive gap discovery.

Each trigger has a generation rule and coverage proof in `raw/candidate-trigger-registry.tsv`. Every emitted canonical candidate must receive a final disposition before candidate-universe closure. Mathematical subsets that no audited trigger emits are placed in the rule-level `NO_GOVERNED_TRIGGER` complement. That complement is a scope proof, not a claim that the excluded concepts have no historical relationship. A newly discovered source pattern invalidates the complement until triggers and ledgers are regenerated.

The research schema deliberately has no inherited maximum arity. Discovery remains finite because triggers emit finite, source- or product-bounded sets. The inherited eight-node and degree-two settings are product/topology parameters whose authority must be audited independently. A source-supported group outside a later product bound must remain an intact research association; it may not be truncated or projected into pairs to satisfy a renderer.

## Canonical identity

A candidate semantic key is the SHA-256 of canonical JSON containing:

- association kind;
- exact vocabulary and bounded-sense IDs;
- whether order is meaningful;
- supported participant positions and roles;
- stable identifiers for bounded time, geography, institution, actors, case, mechanism, and contextual scope.

For generic unordered proximity, participant records are sorted by vocabulary ID, sense ID, role, and participant authority. Incidental source ordering is discarded. When roles or order are evidenced and governed, their positions remain part of identity. Search rank, retrieval order, UI category, render seed, and file order never participate in semantic identity.

Machine validation must additionally prove that declared arity equals participant count, participant sense IDs are unique and resolve through the crosswalk, partial-order constraints are acyclic, and all role bindings use governed stable role IDs. Topology is realization metadata and never part of historical association identity.

Candidate IDs use `R16B-CAND:<sha256>`. A governed active or inquiry association receives a separate stable `R16B-ASSOC:<sha256>` identity only after review. Its append-only `association_revision_id` binds evidence, disposition, review authority, qualifications, non-claims, uncertainty, activation, and product eligibility. Labels, free-text wording, evidence state, reviewer, timestamp, and version never change the stable semantic association ID. A scope-changing correction creates a new association identity linked by split, merge, or supersession provenance. Overlapping member sets are not duplicates when case, stable scope, sense, role, or order differs.

The method requires a new `participant_sense_id` crosswalk because existing rounds do not share one stable sense identifier: Round 9 uses `REL-CAND-*#SENSE-*`, Round 13 can use split IDs, Round 14 assessments use labels, and Round 16A uses `TRV:` vocabulary IDs plus prose senses. `raw/concept-sense-crosswalk.tsv` is deliberately empty in this method checkpoint. Populating and independently verifying it is a closure-blocking candidate-census task; labels alone may not be used as identity.

## Evidence and disposition

Titles, keywords, search rank, metadata co-occurrence, archive categories, database joins, and source-family identity can trigger review. They cannot support an association.

Supporting review requires a stable evidence-bearing locator and one of three final support modes:

- `DIRECT_HIGHER_ORDER_SUPPORT`: the source treats the exact concept set as one bounded configuration;
- `COHERENT_COMPOSITE_SUPPORT`: a documented compatible same-case bundle supports the complete configuration without an unsupported bridge;
- `MIXED_DIRECT_AND_COMPOSITE_SUPPORT`: direct group evidence and compatible composite evidence jointly support it.

The non-supporting and inquiry dispositions preserve materially different outcomes: pairwise support without group coherence, inquiry-only/unresolved value, insufficient evidence, co-occurrence only, bounded-sense or scope conflict, topology or role conflict, and hard negative. `PENDING_GOVERNED_REVIEW` is explicitly nonfinal and never active.

A same-case bundle must document compatibility across case identity, time, geography, institution, actors, mechanism, and sense. Every synthesis step names its evidence premises, bounded inference, and qualification. Combining unrelated pair sources into a global narrative is an unsupported bridge and blocks support.

## Global coherence gate

Every candidate and every product-visible composition receives a review independent of pair admission and renderability. The review covers:

- exact bounded senses;
- one coherent case or an explicitly justified bundle;
- compatible time, geography, institution, actors, and mechanism;
- supported roles and order;
- topology as actually warranted by the evidence;
- decorative or incidental members;
- negative and conflicting evidence;
- unsupported narrative bridges;
- need to split or merge;
- product eligibility and realization.

An internal hard-negative pair prohibits creating that pair claim. It does not automatically defeat a separately evidenced hyperedge. Conversely, even a complete active pair clique remains non-supporting at group level until this gate passes.

## Activation invariants

An association can be product-active only when all of the following are complete and passing:

- governed candidate identity;
- locator-bearing evidence review;
- source access and rights review;
- bounded-sense and contextual scope review;
- counterevidence and falsification review;
- global coherence review;
- final authority decision;
- explicit product eligibility and realization path.

Pending human review, metadata-only discovery, rights/access blocks, unresolved sense, or unresolved coherence always fail closed. Generic proximity is the maximum semantic strength unless governing evidence explicitly supports a stronger typed claim. No causal, directional, chronological, hierarchical, influence, similarity, equivalence, or transitive claim is inferred.

## Required adversarial cases

The candidate census and independent verifier must include at least:

- all internal pairs active while the group is globally invalid;
- a valid hyperedge with sparse or disconnected internal pair projection;
- one participant carrying a conflicting bounded sense;
- a bundle crossing incompatible historical cases;
- an isolated active concept in a valid higher-order association;
- a renderable composition with no valid group association;
- a hyperedge incorrectly projected into internal pair edges;
- an association marked active while evidence review is pending.

These cases test incidence semantics and governance. They are not scholarly evidence.

## Recursive closure discipline

Before any closure attempt, the gap audit is rerun against arity, structural class, trigger dependence, isolated concepts, source patterns, group coherence, exclusions, metadata-only decisions, product reachability, verifier independence, and historiographic defensibility. A new gap is appended, the method or model is expanded, affected artifacts and tests are regenerated, and a new additive checkpoint is published.

This method checkpoint records unresolved closure blockers in `raw/recursive-gap-ledger.tsv`. Therefore no Function 3 closure claim is permitted here.

The trigger registry is a governed method contract, not yet an executable census. `raw/candidate-trigger-occurrence-ledger.tsv`, `raw/association-evidence-ledger.tsv`, and `raw/candidate-exclusion-ledger.tsv` are deliberately empty templates. The next tranche must implement selectors, populate occurrences, and independently prove that no emitted obligation disappears.
