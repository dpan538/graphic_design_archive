# Round 16A baseline correction and reconciliation contract

Round 16A remains immutable evidence of pair-association and pair-derived computational enumeration. Round 16B does not revise or delete its artifacts. It adds a new semantic layer and an explicit reconciliation.

The baseline plan in `raw/round16a-baseline-reconciliation-plan.tsv` freezes twelve headline measurements and their Round 16B treatment. In particular:

- 465 is the complete unordered pair universe for 31 active concepts, not the complete association universe;
- 21 is the active pair-association count, not the final all-arity association count;
- 58 is the count of canonical connected pair-edge subgraphs, not validated historical groups;
- 81 is the count of admitted topology realizations, not association identities;
- 228 is the production-composition count, not an evidence-bearing group count;
- state, transition, workflow, and export counts are products of the v2 binary visibility and navigation rules.

Every Round 16A multi-node result must later be classified as retained and independently validated, retained with corrected provenance, represented through a higher-order association, split, merged, inquiry-only, or rejected with a documented reason. All eleven legacy compositions remain visible in that reconciliation, including the four Round 16A topology-shape rejections. A topology-shape decision is not reused as a historical coherence decision.

The v2 read model and API remain unchanged until a separate additive model checkpoint defines incidence semantics. New higher-order behavior will use a new contract version; it will not silently widen the v2 two-endpoint schema.

At this checkpoint the reconciliation is a plan, not a completed census. The open gap ledger therefore blocks all higher-order, global-coherence, product-reachability, computational-space, and Function 3 closure claims.
