These denominators are release facts. They establish a substantial index and a material evidence-quality gap; they do not establish a representative history or TRACE readiness. The unsafe 9,393 old edge-array rows remain excluded [SR29].

## Decision

`CORPUS_GROWTH_RECOMMENDATION=STOP_UNTARGETED_GROWTH_CLEAN_AND_MEASURE_FIRST`.

There is no two-week value in adding 4,077 records merely to reach 20,000. Collection/digitisation selection can amplify archival silence, and interface/API outputs can create different apparent geographic distributions even from the same collections [SR19][SR20]. Growth must be a documented intervention in a measured quality gap, not a success metric.

| Workstream | Decision | Acceptance condition |
|---|---|---|
| Broad acquisition to 20,000 | STOP | No count target is a release or TRACE gate. |
| v48 relation recovery/automatic inference | STOP | Legacy arrays remain orphaned; no splitting/zipping. |
| Source-family/institution registry | GO, P0 | Every source-family statistic resolves through a governed registry; unknowns retained. |
| Faceted missingness ledger | GO, P0 | Denominators and `UNKNOWN` by source family, era/date precision, place, medium/type, institution/custody, rights and evidence tier. |
| Evidence-tier cleaning pilot | GO-NARROW | Stratified batch with source, review and reversal logs; no extrapolation. |
| Targeted new intake | CONDITIONAL | Named measured gap; independent source path; improves documented quality metric; all missingness/rights states retained. |

## Growth decision indicators

Continue targeted intake only if it improves a release-published measure for a declared underserved stratum. Stop and clean first if provider identity is unknown; the dominant source family grows without a gap rationale; dates/places are coerced; vocabulary is unmapped; unknowns are hidden; or intake reduces verified share. Track top-1/top-3 source-family concentration, evidence-tier rates, unknown dates/places/mediums, controlled-term mapping rate, source/custody diversity, rights-statement and resolvable-source-link rates, plus accepted/held claim metrics. These are quality-control measures—not claims of demographic/historical representation [SR20][SR29].

## Immediate conclusion

Keep `TARGET_20000_IS_ACCEPTANCE_GATE=false`. A smaller, traceable corpus with documented source breadth and higher verified evidence coverage is stronger than a larger count concentrated in unknown-tier records. Current distribution values by geography, era, source family, medium and institution are **UNKNOWN**, not zero; they must be measured before public coverage claims.
