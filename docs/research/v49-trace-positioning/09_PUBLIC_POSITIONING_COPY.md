# Public positioning copy

## 50-word project introduction

`graphic_design_archive` is a release-pinned research index for modern graphic design. It helps readers move from corpus coverage and curated research pathways to object-level sources, provenance and missingness. TRACE currently publishes no accepted historical relations: shared metadata, visual proximity and membership are not evidence of influence.

## 150-word research introduction

`graphic_design_archive` is a Digital Humanities research-infrastructure case study for design history. It tests an evidence-bounded navigation model: Global Atlas reports release-specific corpus coverage and missingness; Evidence Constellation shows explicitly editorial research-path membership; Object Trace leads to object-level source, claim, qualification and provenance records. The model keeps object, evidence occurrence, historical claim, semantic relation, review decision and interface projection separate. It is deliberately fail-closed: visual similarity, co-occurrence, place, date, medium and layout cannot become historical influence. The present release contains 15,923 catalogued objects, of which 7,995 are descriptive-research eligible; 7,928 are held and zero objects are eligible for accepted TRACE relations. It is therefore a research-navigation preview, not an influence map. Images, where linked, remain supplied by external providers and are not represented as locally held or cleared for reuse. The project’s research question is how transparent evidence, provenance and missingness can improve exploratory design-history access.

## Machine/API description

Read-only, release-pinned research-index data for catalogued graphic-design records. Responses expose source/provider provenance, availability/rights state, coverage/missingness and explicit evidence status. This release has `accepted_relation_count: 0`; consumers must not derive historical relations from shared metadata, membership, visual similarity or layout.

## Forbidden promotional language

- “An influence map/network of modern graphic design.”
- “Discovers hidden connections” or “automatically infers influence.”
- “Comprehensive,” “global,” “representative,” “authoritative,” “museum-grade,” or “peer-reviewed.”
- “15,923 unique works” or “20,000 is the quality threshold.”
- “Rights-cleared/open image archive,” “free to reuse,” “preserves the collection,” or “AI-training ready.”
- “Accessible,” “production-ready” or “reproducible” without linked verification for that exact claim.

## Recommended title

Keep the project name if desired, but use the subtitle **“Evidence-bounded research navigation for graphic-design history”**. In the current release name the feature **“TRACE Preview — Evidence Navigation”**; do not use `Influence Map`. Future relation terminology is conditional on a released, reviewed evidence corpus.
